:class:`mne.preprocessing.ICA` gained a ``converged_`` attribute recording whether the decomposition converged or stopped at ``max_iter``, for all four backends. It is written to and read from ``-ica.fif`` files, so a truncated decomposition can be identified after the fact rather than only from a warning at fit time. :func:`mne.preprocessing.infomax` gained a matching ``return_converged`` parameter, by `Aslan Satary Dizaji`_.
